# DESIGN.md — Nexus Observatory

## Surface
Private Operate dashboard. Not marketing. Not `/nexus`.

## Scene
A quiet late-night work desk. The operator watches a living system without theatrical glow.

## Color
Misma materia que Anto Nexus esencial, tema oscuro. Carbón `#101216`, superficie `#191D24`, acento periwinkle `#A5A4FF`. Swatches de 8 px, sin bordes neón. Sin teal `#1adddb` ni navy `#030a24`. El wordmark «Nexus» no aparece en la interfaz.

## Type
System / SF / Inter. Fixed product scale, not fluid display type.

## Layout
Top simulation banner. Header with status. Sidebar on desktop, bottom tabs under 860px. First viewport is the summary band, not a hero.

## Motion
150–250 ms state changes. Live pipeline uses status, not decoration. `prefers-reduced-motion` disables pulse.

## Provenance
Every datum is `simulated`, `unavailable`, or `derived`. The banner **Modo simulación** is permanent in this version.
